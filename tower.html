Project Description

This interactive model visualizes the Fractional Distillation of crude oil. Crude oil is heated in a furnace before entering the bottom of the fractionating column as a mixture of liquid and vapor.

The tower has a temperature gradient—it is hottest at the bottom and coolest at the top. As vapors rise through the tower, they cool and condense back into liquids when they reach a section that is below their specific boiling point. Smaller molecules with lower boiling points rise higher in the tower, while larger molecules with higher boiling points condense lower down. Click on the individual layers to explore the specific hydrocarbons separated at each stage.
